# Code of Conduct

SwiftCoder contributors must communicate professionally and treat other people
with respect. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing,
and deliberate disruption are not accepted in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its participants.
Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, recurrence, and the need
to protect affected people.

Report conduct concerns privately using the contact in `SECURITY.md`. Include
enough context for the maintainers to investigate. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as reasonably possible; retaliation against a reporter is not
accepted.
